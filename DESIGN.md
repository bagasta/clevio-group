# PT Clevio, Direction v4

Source direction: user-provided desktop/mobile drafts, Clevio Group brand guideline, Clevio AI Staff brand guideline, and Clevio AI Pro logo.

Reading this as: corporate group landing page for learners, families, teams, and partners, in a clear human-centered technology style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Decisions and reasons

- Color: the parent brand uses official blue #22367B, white, and black; lime #9DC83B is the only additional color and appears in the supplied Clevio leaf mark plus restrained emphasis.
- Sub-brand color: AI Staff and AI Pro use their white logo variants over photography, with a dark contour shadow for clear separation. Innovator uses its supplied lockup. The Innovator action uses official lime with dark text for contrast.
- Typography: the uploaded Clevio guideline calls for Gotham Narrow, while AI Staff specifies Poppins for headings and Inter for body. The website currently uses Manrope for UI copy, and the supplied wordmark/logo assets preserve the product lettering where brand typography matters most.
- Identity: header and footer use the supplied Clevio leaf-and-wordmark artwork with its checkerboard background removed to transparency; the footer places it on white for contrast. Innovator uses the supplied Innovator Camp lockup. AI Staff and AI Pro use their requested white variants.
- Hero: the supplied blue Clevio Group lockup replaces the repeated text heading, followed by the mission-aligned statement “Memberdayakan manusia melalui teknologi untuk kebaikan bersama.”
- Product marks: each group logo has no background plate. White AI Staff and AI Pro marks use a controlled dark contour shadow over desktop photography. On mobile, their supplied black variants appear at the top of the adjacent copy column above the description, keeping the marks crisp without obscuring the smaller photo or crowding the card.
- Photography: existing service images remain contextual photography and receive a monochrome treatment plus a blue overlay so incidental photo colors do not become UI accents.
- Principles: semantic inline SVG symbols use one stroke weight and Clevio blue to replace font-dependent text glyphs.
- Background: restrained blue-tinted arcs carry over the ecosystem cue from the supplied drafts without adding another accent hue.
- Layout: desktop keeps the three service choices side by side; mobile changes the cards to compact image-and-copy rows for scanability.
- Responsive floor: the page has no fixed minimum viewport width. Below 300 px, the header removes its secondary group action and each service card becomes one full-width vertical unit, preventing horizontal clipping in constrained device emulation.
- Motion: menu, hover, and in-page navigation transitions stay subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
