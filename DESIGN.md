# PT Clevio, Direction v1

Source direction: modern-tepercaya dengan energi teknologi yang lebih terasa.

Reading this as: a corporate narrative site for prospective partners and visitors, in a modern, editorial technology language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Decisions and reasons

- Color: navy establishes institutional trust; one cobalt accent gives actions and navigation a clear technology cue without turning the page into a generic AI gradient.
- Typography: a refined serif is reserved for the corporate statement while a precise sans-serif keeps long-form information legible and current.
- Layout: the page moves from group identity, to its three entities, to an honest company-information boundary so visitors understand the relationship before seeking details.
- Spacing: generous vertical breaks distinguish parent-company information from individual-company identifiers, avoiding a product-card template.
- Surfaces: bordered panels organize the three named entities as a group, with different emphasis rather than three identical marketing cards.
- Motif: three parallel cobalt lines recur as a quiet structural signature, representing the three companies without inventing logos or illustrations.
- Motion: only navigation, theme, and menu feedback animate; this supports orientation and respects a corporate reading context.
- Assets: no logo, people imagery, testimonials, statistics, or product screenshots are used because none were supplied. The wordmark and labelled information placeholders are intentional.
